Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions alone: story first, in order, then the activities and collectibles, then the combat and money grinds, then the 100% cleanup. Nothing here needs a second playthrough if you follow the missable flags.

    Missable trophies — do these in the story, in order

    Every trophy below is flagged missable because its trigger is a story mission, a stranger strand, or a partner moment that may not come back after the finale. This is inferred from the official descriptions, not verified in the game: if the game offers mission or chapter replay, a missed one can be recovered there. Everything not listed here can be earned after the story.
